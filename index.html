Priyanka Kamath

Software Development Manager III, Amazon · Vancouver, BC

Priyanka Kamath is a Software Development Manager III at Amazon in Vancouver, British Columbia. She leads the engineering teams that sit between Amazon's ambition to launch in new markets and the regulatory reality of doing so legally. Her teams built the software that let Amazon launch in Poland, the Netherlands, and Sweden, kept AWS Marketplace compliant across Europe, and replaced manual anti-money-laundering review with machine learning.

She has carried that same rigor into adjacent domains — scaling a security patching product to 5MM+ AWS customers, and now building AI-native systems into how 400K+ Amazon employees experience compensation. As part of the 2026 Annual Compensation cycle, her team shipped Conversation Coach and Compensation Explainability for managers, compensation consultants, and HR partners.

Earlier in her career she built the analytics behind Amazon's Brand Registry program, helping it grow into the backbone of a multi-billion-dollar seller business, and led a 25-person cross-functional organization covering regulatory reporting, denied party and PEP screening, and suspicious activity monitoring across Amazon Stores, AWS Marketplace, and Amazon Gift Cards.

She holds an M.S. in Computer Science from the University at Buffalo and a B.E. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering from NMAM Institute of Technology. Full experience and education →
