Priyanka Kamath

Technology & Risk Leader · Software Development Manager, Amazon · Vancouver, Canada

Priyanka Kamath is a technology leader with 10+ years leading technology risk functions — regulatory compliance, AI risk, and trust — at global technology companies including Amazon, Flexport, and Wipro. She operates at the intersection of risk strategy, governance, product, and technical leadership, helping organizations ship transformative technology while protecting users, developers, and enterprises from emerging and evolving threats.

Today she leads a lean engineering team owning core components of the Amazon & AWS Compensation product, which serves 1M+ Amazon employees, and owns the roadmap to integrate AI natively into the Compensation Calculations platform. For the 2026 Annual Compensation cycle, her team shipped Manager Conversation Coach and Compensation Explainability — used by 400K+ Amazonians across managers, compensation consultants, and HR partners.

Her earlier work set the compliance and security posture behind some of Amazon's highest-stakes systems: a 25-person organization covering worldwide regulatory reporting, denied party and PEP screening, and suspicious activity monitoring across Amazon Stores, AWS Marketplace, and Amazon Gift Cards; the regulatory software that unblocked launches in Poland, the Netherlands, and Sweden; machine learning models that replaced manual anti-money-laundering review; and the security strategy for AWS Systems Manager patching products protecting 5MM+ customer workloads.
