Priyanka Kamath has spent the last decade building and leading engineering teams at Amazon and AWS, mostly in places where the software has to be right the first time: regulatory reporting, financial crime screening, cloud security patching, and employee compensation. She has managed organizations of up to 25 people, taken zero-to-one products to production, and turned manual, rule-driven compliance work into machine learning systems.

She started as a business intelligence engineer on Amazon's Brand Registry program and moved into engineering leadership, running teams across Seattle, Arlington, and Vancouver. Today she leads the team building AI-native features into Amazon's compensation platform.
